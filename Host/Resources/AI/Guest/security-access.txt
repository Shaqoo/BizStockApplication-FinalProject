BizStock Security & Account Recovery 🔐

If you have lost access to your account, authenticator app, or recovery codes:

1. Try logging in with your recovery code (if you saved it).
2. If you don’t have your recovery code or authenticator app:
   - Visit the Lost Access Request page:
     http://localhost:5500/general/lostAccess.html
   - Fill out the form with your registered email and ID details.
   - Our admin team will review your request and contact you via email.

⚠️ Important:
- For your security, we will never ask for your password by email.
- Always keep your recovery codes in a safe place.
- If you regain access, update your recovery codes immediately.

Need more help? Visit the full Help Center:
http://localhost:5500/general/helpcenter.html
